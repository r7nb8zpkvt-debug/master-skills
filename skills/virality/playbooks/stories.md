# Stories (Instagram)

From Jakeschincariol/instagram-agent-skill `/ig-story` (MIT, same author). Read this when the
user asks what to put on their story, wants a story sequence or a poll idea, or wants to sell
without making a post about it.

The feed is how strangers find you. Stories are how followers decide whether you are worth buying
from, and the one surface where a tap becomes a conversation in one move. Only followers see them,
so the job is depth, not reach.

## The daily shape

3 to 7 frames. Past about seven, tap-forwards climb and the last frames play to nobody, which is
exactly where people put the ask.

```
1     OPEN     something happening today, with a face or a hand in it
2-3   MIDDLE   the actual content: the process, the result, the mistake
4     ASK      one sticker: poll, question box, quiz or link
5     CLOSE    the answer, the outcome, or tomorrow's setup
```

## Stickers

| sticker | job | use when |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Poll | the cheapest tap there is | you want volume of response |
| Question box | the audience's exact words | you need ideas or objections, verbatim |
| Quiz | teach by letting them be wrong | the niche has a common misconception |
| Slider | vibe check | nothing important |
| Link | the only clickable surface besides the bio | there is somewhere real to go |
| Countdown | a deadline people can subscribe to | a launch, a live, a close date |
| Add yours | occasional reach past followers | anyone in the niche can answer the prompt |

Question-box answers are hooks written by the audience. Feed them to the hook step as The Question
or The Objection from `tools/hooks.json`, verbatim.

## Rules

- A face or a hand in frame 1. Text on a coloured background gets tapped through.
- One idea per frame.
- On 1080x1920 keep text between y 250 and y 1600: the profile row sits on top, the reply bar below.
- Talk to one person, "you" singular.
- Never repost a feed post with no comment. Say what happened in its comments and why it is worth
  going back for.
- Sell here, not in posts: three frames of context, one of offer, one of proof.

## The DM funnel, honestly

A story that names a problem, a poll or question box that lets people say "that's me", then a
reply to each person who answered. They spoke first. Keyword auto-replies through Instagram's own
tools or an approved partner are supported; bulk DMs to people who did not interact are what get
accounts restricted. They act first, then you reply.

## Output

```
STORIES  ·  Tuesday  ·  5 frames
1  [selfie, walking]    "Third refund request this year. Same reason."
2  [screen recording]   the clause, highlighted
3  [talking]            "Approval is a feeling. Delivery is a date."
4  [poll]               "Have you been burned by a late approval?"  Yes / Not yet
5  [text on photo]      "Reply and I'll send you the clause."
After: everyone who votes Yes gets a reply. That is the whole funnel.
```

Humanize the text first. Nothing is posted. The user posts it.
